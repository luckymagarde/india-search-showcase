## Evidence boundaries
Web-search evidence is directional, not a country-controlled Google SERP/Autocomplete/PAA export or paid keyword database. No search volume, keyword difficulty, rankings or traffic was verified. Buyer questions below are editorial candidates, not claimed PAA queries. Native-language research and actual language-delivery capabilities must be verified before claiming them. Competitor pages are terminology evidence only; do not copy their text or reuse unverified results.
